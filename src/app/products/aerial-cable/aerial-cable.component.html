LT XLPE Aerial Bunched Cables (ABC) are IS-14255 compliant, featuring hard-drawn Aluminium conductors with XLPE/PE insulation and phase identification ridges. Designed for overhead power distribution, they offer reliability, safety, reduced power losses, and cost-effective installation. Ideal for LT distribution lines. ABC cables prevent power theft and are widely used in rural, urban, and space-restricted areas, ensuring stable voltage and superior conductivity.

Key Features

  • Standard: IS-14255 compliant
  • Insulation: XLPE/PE applied using the extrusion process for improved durability
  • Conductor Types:
    • Phase/Street Lighting Conductor: Hard-drawn Aluminium (H2/H4 grade, IS: 8130 standards)
    • Messenger Conductor: Aluminium Silica and Magnesium Alloy (IS: 398 Part 4)
  • Identification of Phases: The phase conductors are identified by ridges on the insulation:
    • 1 ridge for the 1st phase
    • 2 ridges for the 2nd phase
    • 2 ridges for the 2nd phase
    • 3 ridges for the 3rd phase
    • The neutral conductor, if required, can have 4 ridges for easy identification.
  • Colour of Insulation: To protect against UV degradation from exposure to sunlight, the insulation is mixed with carbon black, preventing deterioration of polyethylene and ensuring longevity.
  • Assembling: The phase conductors, along with the lighting conductor (when applicable), are twisted around the messenger conductor, ensuring a compact and secure construction.

Cable Construction & Specifications

  • The phase conductor can be single-phase or three-phase, depending on the specific application.
  • The lighting conductor can be incorporated to supply street lighting.
  • The messenger conductor supports the weight of the cable and maintains tension in the assembly.
  • The phase conductor consists of concentrically stranded aluminium wires (7 or 19 wires).
  • The messenger conductor is made of a specially treated silica, magnesium, and aluminium alloy with 7 wires for added durability.
  • The messenger conductor can be either bare or insulated, based on the customer’s requirements.
  • In some cases, a separate neutral conductor can be provided, though the messenger conductor typically acts as the neutral path.
