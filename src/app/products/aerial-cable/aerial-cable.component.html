LT Aerial Bunched Cables (ABC) are IS-14255 compliant, featuring hard-drawn Aluminium conductors with XLPE/PE insulation and phase identification ridges. Designed for overhead power distribution, they offer reliability, safety, reduced power losses, and cost-effective installation. Ideal for LT distribution lines. LT Aerial Bunched Cables prevent power theft and are widely used in rural, urban, and space-restricted areas, ensuring stable voltage and superior conductivity.

Key Features

  • Standard: IS-14255:1995 with Latest Amendments compliant
  • Insulation: XLPE/PE applied using the extrusion process for improved durability
  • Conductor Types:
    • Phase/Street Lighting Conductor: Hard-drawn Aluminium (H2/H4 grade, IS: 8130:1984 with Latest Amendments standards)
    • Messenger Conductor: Aluminium-Magnesium-Silicon type Alloy (IS: 398- Part 4 :1979 Part 4 with Latest Amendments)
  • Identification of Core: The phase conductors are identified by ridges on the insulation:
    • 1 ridge for the 1st phase
    • 2 ridges for the 2nd phase
    • 3 ridges for the 3rd phase
    • The neutral conductor, if required, can have 4 ridges for easy identification.
    • The Street is without any ridges
  • Colour of Insulation: Black. To protect against UV degradation while exposure to sunlight, the insulation is mixed with carbon black, preventing deterioration of polyethylene and ensuring longevity.
  • Assembling:The phase conductors, along with the lighting conductor (when applicable), are twisted around the messenger conductor, ensuring a compact and secure construction.

Cable Construction & Specifications

  • The phase conductor can be single-phase or three-phase, depending on the specific application.
  • The lighting conductor can be incorporated to supply street lighting.
  • The phase and street light conductor consist of concentrically stranded compacted aluminium wires.
  • The messenger conductor supports the weight of the cable, maintains tension in the assembly and acts as a neutral path and can be either bare or insulated, based on the customer’s requirements.
  • The messenger conductor is made of a Aluminium-Magnesium-Silicon type Alloy wires for added durability.
