Our Aluminium Conductor are engineered for high-performance electrical installations, offering an optimal balance between durability, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. These cables are designed with high-quality aluminium conductors, ensuring reliable power transmission across a variety of applications, including industrial, commercial, and residential projects.

Key Features

  • Conductor Material: High-quality aluminium, ensuring excellent conductivity and lightweight construction for ease of handling and installation.
  • Standard Compliance: Manufactured as per industry standards, including IS: 8130, IS: 398 (Part 4), ensuring optimal performance and reliability.
  • Identification: Phase conductors can be easily identified through color coding or ridges on the insulation, ensuring correct installation and maintenance.

Construction

  • Conductor Type: The conductor is made of hard-drawn aluminium, designed to provide maximum conductivity and strength. Available in a range of grades, including H2 and H4, according to customer requirements.
  • Stranding: The aluminium conductor is concentrically stranded with multiple wires (typically 7 or 19 wires) to enhance flexibility, conductivity, and mechanical strength.

Advantages

Lightweight and Durable

Aluminium conductors are lightweight, making them easier to handle and install, while still maintaining exceptional mechanical strength.

Cost-Effective

Aluminium offers a cost-effective alternative to copper, making these cables a budget-friendly option for large-scale power distribution systems.

Energy Efficient

These cables offer excellent conductivity, ensuring efficient power transmission with minimal energy loss.

Long-Lasting Performance

The aluminium conductors are corrosion-resistant and perform well under various environmental conditions, providing a long service life with minimal maintenance.

Applications

  • Power Distribution Networks: Ideal for use in medium and low-voltage transmission lines.
  • Overhead Power Lines: Providing an efficient and reliable means of delivering power across large areas.
  • Industrial and Commercial Applications: Used for wiring and power distribution within factories, commercial buildings, and other large-scale facilities.
  • Residential Wiring: Offering cost-effective and reliable electrical connections in residential buildings.
