All Aluminium Conductors Steel Reinforced (ACSR) consists of stranded or solid steel core enclosed by strands of aluminum. These are made available in broad range of steels varying from as low as 6% to as high as 40 %. The superior strength of ACSR is ideal for overhead ground wires, river crossings, installations involving extra long span. Manufactured using best quality materials, these conductors ensure high tensile strength.
