MANTRAM INDUSTRIES PRIVATE LIMITED is a global leader in delivering reliable, high-quality products that power industries, infrastructure, and communities. Our company philosophy says a customer-centric approach allows us to deeply understand the customer's needs and ensure their satisfaction, health, and safety standards. We believe in modernization and compliance with the changing requirements of industry. We ensure the quality of product performance while using advanced technology to ensure durability, safety, and efficiency.

Trusted across India, our company has built a reputation for meeting customer needs while minimizing environmental impact. As one of the leading cable and wire manufacturers, we understand that product, process, quality, and performance are the pillars of success, and therefore, our aim is to enhance the efficiency of the product that we deliver to our customers with a strong focus on innovation of the product.

Initiatives taken for Sustainable Future

Installed Solar Panels on Office and Warehouse Roofs


Deployed rooftop solar systems that now provide up to 20% of the facility's daytime electricity needs.

LED Retrofit Across Production and Admin Areas


Replaced over 90% of traditional lighting with high-efficiency LEDs, reducing lighting energy consumption by 60%.

Rainwater Harvesting System for Cooling or Utility Use


Captures rainwater for non-potable use in the plant, reducing dependence on municipal water by 30%.

Reclaimed Copper and Plastic Waste from Production


Recovered and recycled 85% of copper and PVC waste, reintegrating it into the production cycle.

Green Belt Development Around Facility


Planted over 500 native trees and shrubs around the plant boundary, improving biodiversity and air quality.
