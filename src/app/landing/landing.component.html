MANTRAM INDUSTRIES PRIVATE LIMITED specializes in manufacturing high-quality conductors and cables that power industries, infrastructure, and communities. With a strong focus on innovation, the company delivers reliable, efficient solutions tailored to meet the evolving demands of the energy sector. Their commitment to quality assurance and adherence to top industry standards ensures superior product performance, while advanced, technology-driven manufacturing processes enhance precision and efficiency.

Driven by sustainability and customer satisfaction, Mantram Industries integrates eco-friendly practices into its operations, promoting energy-efficient solutions and minimizing environmental impact. With a customer-centric approach and a dedication to responsible manufacturing, the company offers durable, customized products to suit diverse industry needs. Mantram Industries is a trusted partner for those seeking premium, sustainable electrical solutions.

Initiatives taken for Sustainable Future

Installed Solar Panels on Office and Warehouse Roofs


Deployed rooftop solar systems that now provide up to 20% of the facility's daytime electricity needs.

LED Retrofit Across Production and Admin Areas


Replaced over 90% of traditional lighting with high-efficiency LEDs, reducing lighting energy consumption by 60%.

Rainwater Harvesting System for Cooling or Utility Use


Captures rainwater for non-potable use in the plant, reducing dependence on municipal water by 30%.

Reclaimed Copper and Plastic Waste from Production


Recovered and recycled 85% of copper and PVC waste, reintegrating it into the production cycle.

Green Belt Development Around Facility


Planted over 500 native trees and shrubs around the plant boundary, improving biodiversity and air quality.
