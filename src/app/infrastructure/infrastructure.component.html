Detailed Section

Automation & Quality Control

At Matram, we integrate advanced automation and strict quality control at every stage of manufacturing to ensure reliable, high-quality wire and cable products. Efficiency, accuracy, and consistency are enhanced by our automated systems, from wire drawing and annealing to extrusion, jacketing, and robotic assembly. Real-time quality checks are provided by inline monitoring tools such as laser gauges and spark testers to reduce defects and maintain production standards. We conduct testing for purity on raw materials, and we perform in-process and post-production tests on products, including high-voltage, tensile strength, flame retardancy, and aging tests. Full traceability is ensured by bar-coding every coil and drum. We combine intelligent automation with world-class quality assurance to deliver dependable cables that meet international standards for performance, safety, and durability. Clients across power, telecom, and industrial sectors trust us.
