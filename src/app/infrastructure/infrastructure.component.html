Our Infrastructure: The Backbone of Quality

Our manufacturing infrastructure stands as a benchmark in the cable industry, combining scale, technology, and precision. We proudly operate one of the largest and most advanced production facilities for Multistrand Aerial Bunched Cables, with a remarkable monthly capacity of 2,500 kilometers. Alongside this, our plant can produce over 10,000 kilometers of conductors in a wide range of sizes every month, ensuring we can meet diverse and large-scale requirements with ease.


What sets us apart is not just our volume—it’s our commitment to quality and efficiency. Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art, fully automated machinery that drives every stage of the production process, from raw material handling to final testing. This high level of automation minimizes human error, increases throughput, and ensures consistent quality across all product lines.


Our infrastructure is designed to support high-speed manufacturing while adhering to the strictest quality control protocols. With integrated systems for real-time monitoring and precision testing, we deliver products that meet international standards and perform reliably in the most demanding environments.


Whether you're sourcing for a nationwide utility project or a specialized industrial application, our robust manufacturing backbone ensures timely delivery, uncompromised quality, and total peace of mind.
